Common questions

Who is this pilot pay tracker for?

FlightDeck Pay is currently focused on CFIs and flight instructors whose pay is based on logged instructional work.

Can I use it without installing anything?

Yes. It works in the browser, and you can optionally add it to your Home Screen.

Does it replace my employer payroll statement?

No. It is a personal tracking and estimating tool so you can monitor your own hours and expected pay.
