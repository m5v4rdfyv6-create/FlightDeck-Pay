Flight Instructor Pay Calculator & Pilot Pay Tracker

FlightDeck Pay helps CFIs and flight instructors track paid flight, simulator, ground, and miscellaneous hours in one place. Set your own hourly rates, organize entries by pay period, estimate gross and take-home pay, and see how your instructor earnings are building over time.

Use it as a CFI pay calculator for your current paycheck or as an ongoing pilot pay tracker. Your detailed pay entries stay on your device, while an optional account keeps your Pro access available across sessions.
