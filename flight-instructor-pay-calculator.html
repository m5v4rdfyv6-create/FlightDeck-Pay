Common questions

Can I use different flight and ground rates?

Yes. FlightDeck Pay keeps flight/simulator and ground instruction separate so your earnings reflect the rates you actually receive.

Does it track pay over time?

Yes. The current pay period is available in the app, and Pro adds full pay-period history and analytics.

Where is my pay data stored?

Detailed pay entries are stored locally in your browser or installed web app. Account data is used for sign-in and Pro-access status.
